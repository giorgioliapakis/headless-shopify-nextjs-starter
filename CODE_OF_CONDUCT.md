# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone. Be constructive, assume good
faith, respect privacy and identity, and focus criticism on work and evidence rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate exposure of credentials or
merchant data, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces or related private contact.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community or project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled as confidentially and
fairly as possible; retaliation is prohibited.
